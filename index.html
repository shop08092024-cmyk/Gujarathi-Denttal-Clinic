Patient Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about dental procedures and clinic visits.

Is root canal treatment painful? +
Not at all. With modern local anesthesia and advanced rotary endodontic equipment, a root canal procedure at our clinic is virtually painless and feels very similar to a routine tooth filling.
How long does teeth whitening take? +
An in-clinic professional teeth whitening session takes around 45 to 60 minutes. You will see immediate, noticeable results up to 6–8 shades brighter in just one appointment.
Do you treat children? +
Yes! We offer specialized pediatric dental care in a friendly, gentle environment designed to make young children feel completely comfortable and fear-free.
How much does a dental implant cost? +
Dental implant pricing depends on the crown material and bone structure. We offer transparent consultation with no hidden charges, providing precise cost estimates after clinical examination.
Do I need an appointment before visiting? +
While walk-in emergencies are accommodated, we highly recommend booking an appointment in advance via call or WhatsApp to guarantee minimal wait times.
